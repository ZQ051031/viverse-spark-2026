# Spark Drift — 2:30 recording script

Before recording, open the browser build, check that WebGL 2 and sound work, and rehearse the first route. Record at a legible resolution. Have a completed run ready in a second window in case you need a clean cut to the win screen.

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Open on the title, animated island route, and visible how-to-play card. | “This is Spark Drift, a solo 3D arcade flight. You’re the last spark, bringing three island routes back to light.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Point to the instructions, then launch. Show the HUD and briefly pause and resume. | “It cruises automatically. I steer with the arrows or A and D, boost with Up or W, and reverse with Down or S. On touch screens the four arrows work the same way. You need the required shards and a charged portal before time runs out. Lose all three lives or run out of time and the flight ends.” |
| 0:45–1:18 | Play all of Lumen Shoals without cutting. Take the centered first shard, steer left for the next two, then right for the next pair. Show the combo and the portal-charged message; steer back to center and cross the ring. | “These diamonds are shards; the red shapes cost a life. Picking up shards in quick succession increases my score multiplier. That’s five shards, so the portal is ready. I’ll line up with the ring at the end.” |
| 1:18–1:47 | Show the sector-clear screen, click **Fly next sector**, then show Amethyst Run and a short cut to Solstice Rift. | “The next two sectors raise the shard quota, stretch the route, and put moving hazards across your path. Your remaining lives carry over. If I pass a shard, I can reverse for it, but the clock keeps ticking.” |
| 1:47–2:10 | Cross the final charged portal. Hold on **Sky restored** and the score/best. | “That’s all three portals. The result shows this run’s score and the best on this browser. I can fly again immediately to try a cleaner route.” |
| 2:10–2:30 | End on the win screen or cut to the touch HUD. | “Spark Drift runs as a static Three.js game. The islands, effects, and generated audio are original, and playing doesn’t require an account or a download.” |
